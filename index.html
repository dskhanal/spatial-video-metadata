Camera & view
CAVE · Corner angles

Each corner accepts −45° to +45°. Positive angles open the panel outwards (ceiling up, floor down); negative angles fold it inwards. Borders and wall joins follow the geometry. Centre panels stay fixed.

3D Billboard · Stage

A video stage that extends beyond the display. Match your monitor and viewing position, then present full screen. Esc returns to the controls; Space pauses or plays.

The rim stays fixed while depth and angles change. Fit stage explicitly changes its size.

Display & depth

Positive depth brings the video floor in front of the rim. Pop-out stays within 60% of the viewing distance.

Viewpoint

Measure the display width and height, then set your eye position relative to its centre. The perspective is calibrated for that viewing position.

Scene

Drag to look around; scroll to zoom. The sample video is projected onto a rear wall and a foreground floor. This 2.5D profile preserves the perspective already recorded in the people and buildings.

Surface angles

−45° to +45°, hinged at the fixed rim. Positive opens the rear chamber; negative folds inward. Scene depth reduces when needed to keep inward surfaces from crossing.

Manual foreground overlap

Capture a background frame where the selected subject has moved away, then pause at the subject, edit its outline and enable overlap. This uses a still background plate and manual keyframes; a steady camera gives the cleanest result.

Drag the 12 outline points around the subject in the flat editing view. Arrow keys move a focused point. Keep the same scene direction while editing and playing the outline.

Align projector output

For a monitor, keep the automatic alignment. For a projector, show the grid and drag the corners to the physical surface. Presentation hides the grid.
